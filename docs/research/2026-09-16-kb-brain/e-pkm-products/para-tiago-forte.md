# PARA 方法（Tiago Forte / Building a Second Brain）

- 链接：https://fortelabs.com/blog/para/ （"The PARA Method: The Simple System for Organizing Your Digital Life in Seconds"）
- 作者：Tiago Forte（Fortelabs，《Building a Second Brain》作者）
- 时间：2017 首发，持续更新（原文标注 Last updated 2025 左右）

## 原文摘录（保留原文不翻译，资料存档）

> **PARA stands for Projects — Areas — Resources — Archives**, the four top-level categories that encompass every type of information you might encounter in your work and life.

> This isn't a filing system where everything must be perfectly sorted... It's a workflow: information flows between categories as projects progress and priorities shift.

> **Projects** are "a series of tasks linked to a goal, with a deadline"... **Areas** are "spheres of activity with a standard to maintain over time"... **Resources** are "topics or themes of ongoing interest"... **Archives** include "inactive items from the other three categories."

> **The brilliance of PARA is that it doesn't ask what something is, it asks where it goes.** Instead of asking "What category does this belong to?" you ask "**What project will this be used for?**"

> **Organize information by actionability, not by topic.** ... you want to always begin in the most actionable place, which is the current project you're working on.

> Most people, when they want to find a note, ask themselves "Where did I put it?" ... Instead of thinking about where you saved something, think about the **project or goal** you were working on when you saved it.

> Think of it like a series of concentric circles flowing outward: ... **Projects** sit closest to you because they're what you're actively working on... **Resources** are interesting but not directly tied to an action... **Archives** are cold storage.

> **Just-in-time project management**: you don't need to capture everything perfectly up front; you can start with the smallest possible version and elaborate as needed.

## 机制/方法论拆解（中文）

**核心概念**
- **按行动性组织，而不是按主题组织**：P（项目，有目标+截止日）> A（领域，长期责任）> R（资源，持续兴趣）> A（归档，冷却）。信息的存放位置=它服务于哪个行动。
- **流程而非分类**：项目完成→归档；兴趣升温→资源变项目。信息在四层之间流动。
- **检索策略是"按当前项目找"**：写作时不是问"存哪儿了"，而是问"我在做哪个项目时存的"——即知识应挂在工作流（创作）的上下文里。
- **Just-in-time / 渐进式整理**：不追求完美前置整理，允许最小版本起步。

**工作流**：新建项目文件夹 → 所有相关资料按 PARA 落位 → 项目结束时整体归档 → 检索从活跃项目出发。

**为什么有效**：组织成本极低（4 个桶）；与"产出导向"对齐——知识总是服务于正在做的事；降低完美主义瘫痪。

## 局限与批评

- PARA 是**文件管理**方案，不含知识内化机制：不解决"怎么写一张好卡、怎么产生洞见"，只是"放在哪"。社区批评其为"更花哨的文件夹"。
- 按 actionability 归档的知识容易"冻结"在归档层，长期难以复利（与 evergreen 理念相反：evergreen 要求跨项目演化）。
- 对无明确项目的探索型工作（研究、构思）支持弱。
- FtB 书的"CODE 方法论"（Capture-Organize-Distill-Express）中的 Distill（渐进式摘要 progressive summarization）争议大：多层高亮不等于理解。

## 对我们的适用性

1. **"知识挂在创作项目上"是小说工具的天然优势**：我们的知识库应按"当前写作的项目/章节"组织调用面——写作时的知识注入以当前书/章节为第一上下文，而不是全局检索框。
2. **Area/Resource 层对应"领域学习包"**：世界观、写作方法论属于长期 Area，应可跨项目复用；项目结束后沉淀回 Resource，形成流动。
3. **警惕"归档冻结"**：AI 大脑应主动在旧项目归档知识与新作之间架桥（"你三年前研究过这个主题"），避免 RAG 只搜活跃文档。
4. Distill 的教训：**自动摘要不等于学习**——我们的"提炼"必须产出结构化原子知识（可引用、可溯源、可组合），而不是又一层高亮摘要。
