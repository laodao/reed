# Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques（十大学习技法效用评级）

- 链接: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1529100612453266 （全文 PDF: https://www.whz.de/fileadmin/lehre/hochschuldidaktik/docs/dunloskiimprovingstudentlearning.pdf ，已抓取全文）
- 作者: John Dunlosky, Katherine A. Rawson, Elizabeth J. Marsh, Mitchell J. Nathan, Daniel T. Willingham
- 时间: 2013（Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58）
- 来源: SAGE / APS（被引 6000+ 的奠基性系统综述）
- 证据等级: 系统综述（非元分析，但对每种技法做证据强度评级）

## 原文摘录（保留原文不翻译）

> "Practice testing and distributed practice received high utility assessments because they benefit learners of different ages and abilities and have been shown to boost students' performance across many criterion tasks and even in educational contexts."

> "Elaborative interrogation, self-explanation, and interleaved practice received moderate utility assessments. The benefits of these techniques do generalize across some variables, yet despite their promise, they fell short of a high utility assessment because the evidence for their efficacy is limited."

> "Five techniques received a low utility assessment: summarization, highlighting, the keyword mnemonic, imagery use for text learning, and rereading. ... Most students report rereading and highlighting, yet these techniques do not consistently boost students' performance, so other techniques should be used in their place (e.g., practice testing instead of rereading)."

> "8.5 Practice testing: Overall assessment. On the basis of the evidence described above, we rate practice testing as having high utility. Testing effects have been demonstrated across an impressive range of practice-test formats, kinds of material, learner ages, outcome measures, and retention intervals."

> "9.5 Distributed practice: Overall assessment. On the basis of the available evidence, we rate distributed practice as having high utility: It works across students of different ages, with a wide variety of materials, on the majority of standard laboratory measures, and over long delays. It is easy to implement"

> "students mass much of their study prior to tests and believe [blocking is better] ... Simply experiencing the distributed-practice effect may not always be sufficient, but a demonstration paired with instruction about the effect may be more convincing to students"

（对自评"总结"技法的评价）:

> "we rate summarization as low utility. It can [help some students on some criterion tasks, yet the conditions under which these techniques produce benefits are limited]"

## 机制拆解（条件→机制→效果量）

- 十技法按证据强度分三档：高 = 检索练习、间隔练习；中 = 精细询问（elaborative interrogation，追问"为什么"）、自我解释（self-explanation）、交错练习（interleaving）；低 = 总结、高亮、关键词记忆术、文本意象、重读。
- 机制要点：高效技法共同点是"迫使学习者生成/检索"，低效技法共同点是"增加接触的流畅感"（fluency illusion）——流畅 ≠ 学会，重读高亮制造的熟悉感是元认知错觉。
- 值得注意的低效项："总结"（summarization）对学习者要求高（需已具备好的写作/概括技能），否则无效——对我们"自动摘要入库"是重要警示：AI 替用户做总结 = 用户零加工。

## 边界与反例

- 评级基于 2013 前证据；此后 interleaving 与 self-explanation 的教育场景证据增强（后续元分析 d ≈ 0.4–0.6）。
- 低效用 ≠ 无效：summarization 在"培训过如何总结"的学生中有效。
- 高效用技法的边界见 Adesope 条目（先验知识、反馈、格式匹配）。

## 对我们的适用性

1. 产品默认学习动作应映射到"高效用"档：卡片 = 检索练习；dl-pace-scheduler = 间隔；不同领域包的知识交错出题 = interleaving（比按章节顺序刷更有效且证据在增强）。
2. "AI 替用户总结"必须与"用户自己生成"配对：自动提炼的原子知识是输入侧资产，但吸收侧必须让用户自己复述/解释/应用，否则落入 summarization-by-proxy 的零加工陷阱。
3. 概念图（dl_concept）本身在 Dunlosky 评级体系外，但 Karpicke & Blunt 2011 显示"概念图式整理"若不伴随检索，效果低于自由回忆——概念图浏览视图要低优先于"凭记忆重建"交互。
4. 对抗 fluency illusion 的具体 UI：阅读态与自测态视觉上应可区分，避免"刚才看过所以觉得会了"。
5. "演示 + 讲解效应本身"能说服用户接受间隔安排（Balch 2006）——onboarding 时给用户做一次"你自己忘得多快"的小实验，比说明文章有效。
